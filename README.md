Steady Sniper: a paper-trading bot for the pump.fun replay that buys the dip — a young coin down 12–40% from its window high that has started to bounce with buy flow no longer negative — at a fixed 0.25 SOL per position, then exits whole on a +8% take-profit, a −6% stop-loss, or after ~36 minutes held, with a 10-minute cooldown and at most two entries per coin. Built for hourly Sharpe: many small, diversified round trips (121+ on the dev window, 100+ distinct mints) instead of one lucky runner. Momentum chasing and newborn sniping were both tried on the dev window and lost; the dip entry was the only one near breakeven there.

node main.ts
